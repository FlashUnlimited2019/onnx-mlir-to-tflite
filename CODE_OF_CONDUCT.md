# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
environment for everyone participating in this project, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and respect toward other people;
- giving and accepting constructive technical feedback;
- focusing discussion on ideas, evidence, and the needs of the community;
- acknowledging mistakes and learning from them;
- respecting privacy and differing viewpoints.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or threats;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- insulting, derogatory, or inflammatory comments and personal attacks;
- deliberate disruption of project discussions or collaboration;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces. Project spaces include the source
repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project
communication channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report unacceptable behavior privately using the contact information published
on the [project maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/FlashUnlimited2019).
Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Conduct
that also violates GitHub's policies may be reported through GitHub's own
reporting tools.
